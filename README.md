# 🎙️ Open-Wispr - Type documents using your own voice

[![Download Open-Wispr](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Open--Wispr-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Wemerso458/Open-Wispr)

Open-Wispr turns your spoken words into text on your screen. You hold the function key, speak clearly, and the software writes your thoughts into any application. It uses the OpenRouter service to process speech. This tool keeps your workflow fast and prevents repetitive strain from typing.

## ✨ Features

*   **Dictation integration:** Works in every text field on your computer.
*   **Keyboard control:** Starts when you hold the assigned trigger key.
*   **Privacy focus:** Sends only the necessary audio data to your chosen provider.
*   **Custom keys:** Allows you to use your own API key for full control.
*   **Lightweight:** Runs in the menu bar without slowing your machine.

## ⚙️ Requirements

*   macOS 12.0 or newer.
*   A stable internet connection.
*   An active OpenRouter account key.

## 🔑 Setup

You need an OpenRouter account to use the service. Follow these steps to link your account to the software.

1. Sign up for a free account at the OpenRouter website.
2. Navigate to your account settings to generate a new API key.
3. Copy the long string of characters provided.
4. Launch Open-Wispr on your computer.
5. Click the app icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen.
6. Select Settings from the dropdown menu.
7. Paste your API key into the text field labeled Key.
8. Click Save to activate the connection.

## 🗣️ How to use the app

Open-Wispr waits in the background until you need it. 

1. Place your cursor in any text editor, web browser, or document where you need to type.
2. Press and hold the function (fn) key on your keyboard.
3. Speak your sentences clearly. The software captures your voice as long as you hold the key.
4. Release the key when you finish speaking. The app converts your audio to text and inserts it at the cursor position. 
5. Correct any errors directly in your document if necessary.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

If the software fails to type your words, check these common items:

*   **Internet access:** The app requires a connection to reach the processor. Verify your Wi-Fi or wired connection.
*   **Microphone access:** macOS protects your privacy by restricting microphone access. Go to System Settings, then Security & Privacy, and ensure the app has permission to use the microphone.
*   **API Key validity:** If the app signals an error, check that your OpenRouter key remains valid and has enough credits for usage.
*   **System volume:** Check that your microphone functions correctly in other applications like Zoom or Voice Memos.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

This software does not store your voice recordings on your local drive. It forwards the audio to the OpenRouter service for transcription. Because you provide the API key, you decide which service handles your data. You may rotate or delete your API key at any time through the OpenRouter dashboard to stop all access. 

## 💡 Best practices for speech

The technology works best when you follow these tips for clear input:

*   Speak at a natural pace.
*   Maintain a steady distance from the computer microphone.
*   Use a quiet room to reduce background noise from fans or traffic.
*   Pronounce words distinctively to help the linguistic engine understand specific terms.
*   Avoid overlapping voices or chatter during the recording window.

## 📝 Configuration options

The app allows for minor adjustments through the Settings menu:

*   **Language selection:** Choose your primary language to improve machine understanding of grammar and accent patterns.
*   **Startup behavior:** Toggle whether the app opens automatically when you start your computer.
*   **Trigger modification:** Change the key used for dictation if your current keyboard layout requires a different shortcut.
